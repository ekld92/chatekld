Deck Generator

Builds a Beamer .tex deck from your indexed vault, in your own template's house style. Emit-only — it writes a <name>/ project folder you compile with make.
Its preamble (document class, theme, packages, custom macros) is reused verbatim. Load it, then edit below before generating.
A <name>/ folder is created here with <name>.tex + a Makefile. Must sit beside your common/ dir so ../common/… and ../_master.bib resolve.
Max sections, agent iterations and temperature for deck generation live in ⚙ LLM Settings in the sidebar.

Augment an existing deck

Deepen a section, add a table, or insert a new section into an existing Beamer .tex — grounded in your vault. You preview the change and confirm before anything is written.
Any complete Beamer deck — one deckgen produced, or hand-written. The deck/preamble outside the edited part is preserved byte-for-byte.
Uses the deck's own custom macros + bibliography when present, and the Audience/citation settings above. Agent iterations and temperature come from ⚙ LLM Settings.

Report / Article Generator

Builds a LaTeX article/report .tex (prose, not slides) from your indexed vault, in your own template's house style. Emit-only — it writes a <name>/ project folder you compile with make.
Its preamble (document class, packages, custom macros, \addbibresource) is reused verbatim. Load it, then edit below before generating. A plain article/report template works fine.
A <name>/ folder is created here with <name>.tex + a Makefile. If your template pulls in suite files (../common/…, ../_master.bib), place it under your suite root; otherwise any folder works.
Max sections, agent iterations and temperature for document generation live in ⚙ LLM Settings in the sidebar.

Or: a Markdown note in your vault

A vault-relative folder. It is created if it doesn't exist. The note only ever writes inside your vault.

Note Refactor

⚠ Writes to your vault. Phase 1 previews are read-only, but Apply inlines the approved callouts into your notes, and per-image Archive moves a full-res original out of the vault (to a recoverable archive folder) leaving a small thumbnail. Every change is atomic, logged, and reversible from Restore. Nothing is written until you explicitly confirm.
Only this sub-folder's .md notes are analyzed. Attachments are resolved across the whole vault.
Drops the “This image is…/Transcribed Text:” lead-in from every inlined callout (keeps the transcription). Saved to settings; re-runs the plan. Additive to the per-image Strip button.
